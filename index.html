Program overview

Ask better questions.
Find answers in data.

Data science brings together computer science, mathematics and statistics to turn complex information into useful knowledge. At the University of Regina, students learn to explore, prepare, visualize and analyze data—and use it to make informed predictions and decisions.

Through a combination of technical learning and analytical thinking, you can build a foundation for work in technology, business, government, research, health and other data-rich fields.

Explore the subject areas

The program includes computer science, mathematics and statistics courses. Here are examples of courses connected to data science at the B.Sc and M.Sc levels; check the official calendar for current requirements.

B.Sc
CS 265

Introduction to Data Science

Build a foundation for working with data and using analytical methods to find useful information.

B.Sc
CS 465

Data Mining

Study pattern mining, classification, regression, clustering and knowledge discovery from data.

M.Sc
CS 711

Foundations of Data Science (3 cr)

Overview of the data science lifecycle and methods, including data ethics, data discovery and preparation, model planning, machine learning implementation and evaluation, visualization and delivery.

M.Sc
CS 714

Big Data Analytics & Cloud Computing (3 cr)

Big data analytics in the cloud: containers, micro-services, distributed file systems, MapReduce, real-time processing and cloud-based machine learning, with a milestone-based cloud project.

Course descriptions are brief summaries. Course availability and degree requirements may change; consult the official program page and academic calendar before registering.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about studying data science at the University of Regina.

What subjects does data science combine?

Computer science, mathematics and statistics. Students learn to work with data, identify patterns, analyze results and communicate findings.

How long is the BSc in Data Science?

It is listed as a four-year program. Check the official program page for current requirements.

Is there co-op, a minor or undergraduate research?

Options depend on current University and Faculty of Science offerings. Ask an academic advisor and check the official pages.

Can I transfer into the program?

It depends on your previous studies and the University's admission and program requirements. Contact admissions or an academic advisor.

What careers can it lead to?

Technology, business, government, health, research and other fields that use data to inform decisions.
